The Water Bottle, Mine

On November 3, 2025, I left my water bottle at the bus stop in central campus. Three days later, I got a new one.

Over the past year, I drank about 90% of my water from this bottle. It’s a little worn out now and also has a few dents, so it seems like time to get a new one. Yet I know I’m not ready to let this one go. Last week, I thought I lost it for a minute and panicked. That moment gave me this idea to make a digital copy of it, just in case I finally have to say goodbye one day.

The best part about this bottle is the stickers. I don’t have that many but I got them from some places that I visited in the US and Indonesia and they hold memories of my travels.

Drag to turn the bottle. Arrow keys rotate; Home resets the view. Space pauses or resumes the gentle rotation.
